**Project ID:** RA-CTI-001  
**Target:** Allied Bank Limited, Pakistan  
**Purpose:** Track public sources used in cybersecurity research.

## Source S-001 — Allied Bank Annual Report

- **Source type:** Official bank publication
- **URL:** https://www.abl.com/investor-relations/financials/financial-reports/
- **Purpose:** Review publicly disclosed cybersecurity controls and governance.
- **Evidence collected:** Annual-report excerpt describing security assessments, SOC capabilities, MDR, PCI DSS, SWIFT CSP, and ISO 27001:2022.
- **Limitation:** Corporate disclosures do not independently prove the effectiveness of controls or establish the vulnerability status of individual systems.
- **Status:** Initial review completed; exact report page and publication details to be recorded.

## Research Rules

1. Record the date each source was accessed.
2. Prefer official sources and primary evidence.
3. Separate confirmed facts from analysis and hypotheses.
4. Do not attribute a vulnerability to Allied Bank without bank-specific evidence.
5. Record unavailable information as unknown, not as proof that no issue exists.
