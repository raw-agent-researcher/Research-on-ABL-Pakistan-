# RAW AGENT — Allied Bank Pakistan Public CTI Research

**Tagline:** Evidence Over Assumptions

## Project Overview

An independent public-source cybersecurity research project examining publicly available cybersecurity disclosures and relevant threat intelligence relating to Allied Bank Limited, Pakistan.

## Objectives

- Review official cybersecurity disclosures.
- Examine relevant public CERT advisories and vulnerability records.
- Distinguish verified facts from general banking-sector threats.
- Document evidence, uncertainty, and source limitations.
- Develop evidence-based defensive recommendations.

## Methodology

Research will use official bank publications, regulator notices, national CERT advisories, vendor security bulletins, and public vulnerability databases.

Each finding will include source links, dates, evidence, confidence, and limitations.

## Ethical Scope

- Public sources only.
- No unauthorized scanning, exploitation, or access to private systems.
- No collection or publication of sensitive customer information or credentials.
- No unsupported claims that the bank is vulnerable.

## Initial Research Status

The initial source review identified publicly reported cybersecurity controls in the bank's annual-report excerpt. This does not establish the effectiveness of those controls or the vulnerability status of individual systems.

Further public-source verification is required.
